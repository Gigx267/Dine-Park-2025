About Dine & Pack

Project 100

Empowering the Next Generation

Project 100 is an initiative dedicated to empowering children and young adults in the culinary arts, giving them the tools they need to reach their full potential and build sustainable futures.

This project promotes self-reliance and aims to create a level playing field for children across Nigeria and the world. We believe that every child, regardless of background or means, should have the opportunity to learn, grow, and chart their own course—free from dependence on others for their livelihood.

Through Project 100, professional chefs from diverse culinary backgrounds personally coach participants. They are taught not only the art of cooking but also how to start, run, and sustain a culinary business, laying the foundation for a future filled with possibility and independence.

Inspiring. Equipping. Uplifting.

Mini Chef Training

Empowering Young Culinary Talents

Mini Chef Training is a program fueled by our passion for grooming children from a very young age, offering them the opportunity to showcase their talents and present the value of Nigerian cuisine wherever they are in the world.

Our ultimate goal is to equip every child with life skills that foster independence and help them stand out, no matter where they find themselves. By teaching them a valuable trade, we empower them to sustain themselves anywhere in the world.

The Mini Chef Training experience is more than just culinary skills—it's about creating memorable moments through fun and educational activities. Our curriculum includes team bonding exercises like hiking, picnics, and dance workout sessions, as well as mentorship and public speaking classes to motivate the children and boost their self-esteem.

These engaging activities ensure the longevity and continuity of this initiative, helping the next generation of chefs build confidence, creativity, and a lifelong love for culinary arts.

Inspiring Young Minds, One Dish at a Time

Dine & Pack Interschool Cooking Competition

Inspiring Culinary Excellence and Healthy Competition Among Schools

The Dine & Pack Interschool Cooking Competition fosters healthy interaction between schools and students, providing a vibrant platform for schools to thrive and showcase their culinary prowess.

Culinary training and competitions are our forte. We bring together competitors from various schools and age groups to compete in a collegial spirit, producing measurable and enviable results.

Having conceptualized and hosted two editions of this unique competition in the Federal Capital Territory, we remain the pioneers of interschool culinary contests in the FCT and Nigeria at large. Our vision is to take this event nationwide, establishing a National Championship and ultimately making it a global event—putting Nigeria on the world culinary map.

The competition highlights the vital role of the food industry in our economy and daily lives, while educating children on the significance and benefits of culinary arts. Cash prizes, ranging from fifty thousand to five hundred thousand naira, are awarded to improve home economics or related departments in the schools. Every participating school receives both cash and gift prizes, encouraging continuous improvement.

Participants are recognized not only for their cooking skills but also for teamwork, leadership, neatness, and punctuality. We ensure that schools utilize their prizes appropriately, providing them with the equipment needed for ongoing training and students with utensils for home use.

As part of our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), we offer scholarships and mentorship programs to students interested in pursuing culinary careers—covering business management, bookkeeping, accounting, and more. A comprehensive startup package is provided to help them launch their ventures successfully.
